🗺️ Geography · Chapter 01

🌍 The Earth in Which We Live

Continents, Oceans, Latitudes & Longitudes

📖 Key Concepts

Rotation — Earth spinning on its own axis — takes 24 hours (1 day).
Revolution — Earth orbiting the Sun — takes 365 days (1 year).
Latitude — Imaginary horizontal lines drawn West to East on the globe. The equator is 0°.
Longitude — Imaginary vertical lines drawn North to South. The Prime Meridian is 0°.
Equator (0°) — Divides Earth into Northern Hemisphere and Southern Hemisphere.
Prime Meridian (0°) — Divides Earth into Eastern Hemisphere and Western Hemisphere.
Continents (7) — Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.
Oceans (5) — Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern (Antarctic), Arctic.
International Date Line — Follows the 180° longitude; separates one calendar day from the next.

📌 Important Rules & Facts

📌 Latitudes run East–West and measure North or South of the equator (0°–90°).
📌 Longitudes run North–South and measure East or West of the Prime Meridian (0°–180°).
📌 The equatorial diameter of Earth is 12,757 km; polar diameter is 12,714 km — Earth is NOT a perfect sphere.
📌 Total surface area of Earth = 510 million km².
📌 360 longitudes in total — 180° East and 180° West.

💡 Examples

Location using coordinates: Colombo, Sri Lanka is approximately at 6°N latitude, 80°E longitude.
Day and Night: Earth's rotation causes day and night. When Sri Lanka faces the Sun it is daytime.
Seasons: Earth's revolution and tilt cause the four seasons in temperate regions.

🔑 Chapter Summary

Earth rotates (24 h) and revolves (365 days). Latitudes are horizontal; longitudes are vertical. The equator (0° lat) and Prime Meridian (0° lon) divide Earth into hemispheres. Earth has 7 continents and 5 oceans.
