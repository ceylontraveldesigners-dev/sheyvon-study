🇱🇰 Identity of Sri Lanka

Geographical & Social Identity of Sri Lanka

📖 Key Concepts

Location — Sri Lanka is in the Indian Ocean near the equator, between the Tropic of Cancer and Tropic of Capricorn, in the Northern Hemisphere.
Relative Location — Position described using surrounding lands and oceans — separated from India by the Palk Strait.
Specific Location — Described using exact latitudes and longitudes. Sri Lanka lies between 5°55'N–9°51'N and 79°52'E–81°53'E.
Area — 65,610 km². Length (Point Pedro to Dondra Head) ≈ 432 km; width ≈ 224 km.
Shape — Sri Lanka is an island — pear-shaped, surrounded by the Indian Ocean.
Palk Strait — Narrow strip of sea separating Sri Lanka from India.
Nearby Islands — Maldives, Andaman Islands, Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep Islands are also in the Indian Ocean.
Sea Zones — Sri Lanka has Territorial Waters (12 nautical miles), Contiguous Zone (24 nm), Exclusive Economic Zone (200 nm).

📌 Important Rules & Facts

📌 Sri Lanka is closest to the Indian sub-continent, separated by the Palk Strait.
📌 Located in the tropical zone — has a warm, humid climate.
📌 Being an island gives Sri Lanka exclusive rights over large sea areas (EEZ = 200 nautical miles).
📌 Sri Lanka has the third largest EEZ in the Indian Ocean.

💡 Examples

Benefit of location: Sri Lanka's position in the Indian Ocean makes it a major shipping crossroads between East and West.
Sea resources: The EEZ gives Sri Lanka rights to fisheries, oil, and mineral resources up to 200 nautical miles offshore.
Climate: Being near the equator, Sri Lanka has a tropical climate with two monsoon seasons.

🔑 Chapter Summary

Sri Lanka is a tropical island in the Indian Ocean, separated from India by the Palk Strait. Its total area is 65,610 km². Specific location: 5°55'N–9°51'N, 79°52'E–81°53'E. It benefits from rich sea zones including a 200 nm EEZ.
