🔑 What is the Internet?

Internet: A global network of millions of computers connected together, allowing them to communicate and share information. It is the largest computer network in the world.
World Wide Web (WWW): A collection of websites and web pages stored on servers around the world, accessed through the internet using a web browser. The WWW is part of the internet — not the same thing!

🌐 Key Internet Terms

TermMeaning
Web BrowserSoftware used to access websites (e.g. Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
Search EngineA website that helps you find information on the internet (e.g. Google, Bing, Yahoo)
URLUniform Resource Locator — the web address of a page (e.g. www.google.com)
WebsiteA collection of related web pages on the same domain
Web PageA single page on a website (like one page of a book)
HTTP/HTTPSProtocol for transferring data. HTTPS is the secure version (S = Secure)
IP AddressA unique number that identifies each computer on a network (like a postal address)

📧 Email (Electronic Mail)

Email: A way to send electronic messages over the internet. It is fast, free, and can include attachments (files).
Part of EmailMeaning
To:Recipient's email address (who you are sending TO)
CC:Carbon Copy — other people who receive a copy
BCC:Blind Carbon Copy — receives copy but others cannot see their address
Subject:Short title/topic of the email
Attachment:A file sent along with the email (photo, document, etc.)
💡 Email address format: username@domainname.extension
Example: sheyvon@gmail.com

🔍 Using a Search Engine

  • Type keywords (important words) into the search bar
  • Use specific keywords for better results
  • Use quotes for exact phrases: "Sri Lanka history"
  • Results are ranked by relevance
  • Always check that information is from a reliable/trusted source

⚠️ Internet Safety Rules

🛡️ Stay Safe Online:
  • Never share personal information online (full name, address, phone number, school name)
  • Never meet someone you only know from the internet without a trusted adult
  • Use strong passwords for all online accounts
  • Be careful of phishing — fake websites or emails trying to steal your information
  • Tell a trusted adult if you see anything online that makes you uncomfortable
  • Only download from trusted websites to avoid viruses

📡 Types of Internet Connections

  • Broadband (ADSL) — uses phone lines, common in homes
  • Fibre Optic — uses light signals through glass fibres, very fast
  • Wi-Fi (Wireless) — connects devices wirelessly through a router
  • Mobile Data (3G/4G/5G) — connects through mobile phone network
