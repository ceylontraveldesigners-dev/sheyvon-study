🔑 What is Presentation Software?

Presentation Software: Software used to create visual slideshows (presentations) that can include text, images, charts, animations, and videos. Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) is the most common example.

Presentations are used for school projects, business meetings, teaching, and speeches to communicate information clearly and attractively.

💻 The PowerPoint Interface

  • Slide Panel — shows thumbnails (small previews) of all slides on the left
  • Slide Area — the main area where you design each slide
  • Ribbon — tabs with tools: Home, Insert, Design, Animations, Slide Show
  • Notes Panel — below the slide — for presenter notes (audience cannot see these)
  • Status Bar — shows slide number and view options at the bottom

🎨 Creating and Formatting Slides

ActionHow to do it
Add new slideHome tab → New Slide, or right-click slide panel → Insert Slide
Change layoutHome tab → Layout — choose Title Slide, Title & Content, Blank, etc.
Apply Theme/DesignDesign tab → choose a theme (changes colours, fonts, background for all slides)
Insert imageInsert tab → Pictures or Online Pictures
Insert chartInsert tab → Chart — choose bar, pie, line, etc.
Add text boxInsert tab → Text Box — click and drag on slide

✨ Animations and Transitions

Animation: Movement applied to an object (text, image, shape) ON a slide. Example: text flies in from the left when you click.
Transition: The visual effect that occurs when moving FROM one slide TO the next. Examples: Fade, Wipe, Fly In, Morph.
💡 Tip: Use animations and transitions SPARINGLY — too many make the presentation look unprofessional and distract the audience.

▶️ Presenting a Slideshow

  • Start from beginning: Slide Show tab → From Beginning, or press F5
  • Start from current slide: Slide Show tab → From Current Slide, or press Shift + F5
  • Next slide: Click mouse or press spacebar / right arrow
  • Previous slide: Press left arrow / backspace
  • End slideshow: Press Escape (Esc)

💡 Good Presentation Tips

  • Use large, readable font sizes — at least 24pt for body text
  • Keep text short and clear — do not copy entire paragraphs onto a slide
  • Use high-contrast colours — dark text on light background or light text on dark background
  • Include relevant images and diagrams to support the message
  • Limit to one main idea per slide
