Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Step 1 of constructing a perpendicular bisector is:

💡 Open compass past the midpoint, then draw arcs.

Q2. An isosceles triangle has a base of 6cm and equal sides of 5cm. How do you find the apex?

💡 Compass set to 5cm from each endpoint.

Q3. A locus of points 3cm from point P is a:

💡 All points 3cm from P form a circle.

Q4. Constructing 60° angle uses the fact that an equilateral triangle has:

💡 Each angle of equilateral triangle = 60°.

Q5. After constructing perpendicular bisector of AB, what can you measure?

💡 The bisector passes through the midpoint.

Q6. Which construction gives a right angle?

💡 Perpendicular constructions give 90°.

Q7. To construct an angle bisector of angle X: first draw arcs on:

💡 Mark equal arcs on both sides from the vertex.

Q8. A regular hexagon is constructed using a compass set to the radius because:

💡 In a regular hexagon, side = radius.

Q9. The perpendicular bisector of a triangle's side passes through:

💡 The circumcentre is equidistant from all vertices.

Q10. Locus of points equidistant from two parallel lines is:

💡 The midline is equidistant from both parallel lines.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. A compass is used to measure angles in geometric construction.

💡 Compass transfers lengths; protractor measures angles.

Q12. An angle bisector divides an angle into two equal angles.

💡 That is the definition.

Q13. The perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre of a circle.

💡 A key theorem in circle geometry.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The angle bisector of a 70° angle creates two angles of ___° each.

💡 70÷2=35°

Q15. Constructing an equilateral triangle: set compass to the ___ of the triangle.

💡 All sides are equal, so set compass to that length.
