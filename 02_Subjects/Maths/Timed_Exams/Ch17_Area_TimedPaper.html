Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. A room is 5m × 4.5m. Area = ?

💡 5×4.5=22.5 m²

Q2. Triangle: base=14cm, height=9cm. Area = ?

💡 ½×14×9=63 cm²

Q3. A square field has area 225 m². Side = ?

💡 √225=15 m

Q4. Composite: rectangle 10×6 with a 2×3 corner removed. Area = ?

💡 60−6=54 cm²

Q5. Parallelogram: base=8m, slant side=5m, height=4m. Area = ?

💡 8×4=32 m² (use perpendicular height)

Q6. Floor tiles each 30cm×30cm. How many to cover 2.7m×1.8m floor?

💡 2.7m=270cm, 1.8m=180cm. Area=48600cm². Tile=900cm². 48600÷900=54

Q7. Rectangle has perimeter 24cm and width 4cm. Area = ?

💡 l=(24÷2)−4=8; A=8×4=32 cm²

Q8. Which has larger area: triangle (base 8, height 10) or rectangle (5×7)?

💡 Triangle=40 cm²; Rectangle=35 cm². Triangle wins.

Q9. Area of a right triangle with legs 6cm and 8cm:

💡 ½×6×8=24 cm²

Q10. A garden is 15m×8m. A path 1m wide runs along two shorter sides. Area of path = ?

💡 Two 1m×8m paths = 2×8=16 m²

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. The area of a triangle is always half the area of a rectangle with the same base and height.

💡 A=½bh is half of A=bh.

Q12. 1 m² = 100 cm²

💡 1 m² = 10000 cm² (100cm × 100cm).

Q13. Area of a square is found by squaring the side length.

💡 A = s × s = s²

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Area of rectangle 13cm × 4cm = ___ cm².

💡 13×4=52

Q15. A triangle has area 30cm² and base 10cm. Height = ___ cm.

💡 30=½×10×h; h=6
