Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. (−9) + 4 = ?

💡 −9 + 4 = −5.

Q2. 6 − (−8) = ?

💡 6 + 8 = 14.

Q3. (−5) × 3 = ?

💡 Different signs → −15.

Q4. (−12) ÷ (−4) = ?

💡 Same signs → positive: 3.

Q5. Which is greatest: −1, −8, 0, −5?

💡 0 is the greatest.

Q6. (−2)³ = ?

💡 (−2)×(−2)×(−2) = 4×(−2) = −8.

Q7. Temperature drops from 3°C to −7°C. Change = ?

💡 3 − (−7) = 3+7 = 10°C drop.

Q8. (+4) + (−4) = ?

💡 +4 + −4 = 0.

Q9. (−3) × (−3) × (−3) = ?

💡 (−3)²=9, 9×(−3)=−27.

Q10. Arrange in order (smallest first): −3, 5, −8, 1

💡 Smallest to largest: −8, −3, 1, 5.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. The product of two negative numbers is always negative.

💡 (−)×(−) = positive.

Q12. −15 < −3

💡 −15 is further left on the number line, so it is smaller.

Q13. (−6) + 6 = 0

💡 A number plus its negative = 0.
