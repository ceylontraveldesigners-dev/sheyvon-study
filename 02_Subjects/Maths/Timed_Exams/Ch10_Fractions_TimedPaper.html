Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. ¾ − ½ = ?

💡 LCD=4. ¾ − ²⁄₄ = ¼.

Q2. Which fraction is largest: ½, ⅓, ¾, ⅔?

💡 ¾=0.75 is the largest.

Q3. 2½ + 1¾ = ?

💡 2+1=3, ½+¾=¼+1=1¼. Total: 4¼.

Q4. ¾ ÷ ³⁄₈ = ?

💡 ¾ × ⁸⁄₃ = ²⁴⁄₁₂ = 2.

Q5. Simplify ²⁴⁄₃₆.

💡 HCF(24,36)=12. 24÷12=2, 36÷12=3. Answer ⅔.

Q6. What is ²⁄₃ of 30?

💡 30 × ²⁄₃ = 20.

Q7. Convert ¹³⁄₄ to a mixed number.

💡 13÷4=3 remainder 1. So 3¼.

Q8. ½ × ⅔ × ¾ = ?

💡 1×2×3 / 2×3×4 = 6/24 = ¼.

Q9. 3 ÷ ½ = ?

💡 3 × ²⁄₁ = 6.

Q10. Which equals ⅗?

💡 9÷15: HCF=3. 9÷3=3, 15÷3=5. So ⅗.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. An improper fraction is always greater than 1.

💡 ⁵⁄₅=1 is improper but not greater than 1.

Q12. ⅓ + ⅙ = ½

💡 LCD=6. ²⁄₆ + ⅙ = ³⁄₆ = ½.

Q13. To simplify a fraction, divide both parts by their LCM.

💡 Divide by the HCF to simplify.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. ½ ÷ ¼ = ___.

💡 ½ × 4 = 2.

Q15. ¾ of 80 = ___.

💡 80 × ¾ = 60.
