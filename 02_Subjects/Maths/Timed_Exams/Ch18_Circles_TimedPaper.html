Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Circumference of circle with r=21 cm (π=22/7):

💡 C=2×22/7×21=132 cm

Q2. Area of circle with d=14 cm (π=22/7):

💡 r=7; A=22/7×49=154 cm²

Q3. If circumference=62.8 cm (π=3.14), find the radius.

💡 2πr=62.8; r=62.8÷6.28=10 cm

Q4. A circular pond has radius 3.5 m. Area (π=22/7):

💡 A=22/7×12.25=38.5 m²

Q5. A wheel has diameter 70 cm. Distance for 10 full turns (π=22/7):

💡 C=22/7×70=220 cm; 10×220=2200 cm

Q6. Which is larger: area of circle r=5 or square side=10?

💡 Circle=78.5 cm²; Square=100 cm²

Q7. Semi-circle with r=7 cm. Perimeter (π=22/7):

💡 Half circumference + diameter = 22+14=36 cm

Q8. If area=π×64, find the radius.

💡 πr²=π×64; r²=64; r=8 cm

Q9. Diameter of circle with circumference 44 cm (π=22/7):

💡 d=44÷(22/7)=44×7/22=14 cm

Q10. Area of semi-circle r=10 cm (π=3.14):

💡 ½×3.14×100=157 cm²

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. The area of a circle increases 4 times when the radius doubles.

💡 A=πr²; if r→2r, A→π(2r)²=4πr².

Q12. Circumference and area of a circle have the same units.

💡 Circumference: cm; Area: cm².

Q13. A circle with larger diameter has larger circumference.

💡 C=πd; more d → more C.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Radius of circle with diameter 18 cm = ___ cm.

💡 r=18÷2=9 cm

Q15. Circumference of circle with r=7 cm (π=22/7) = ___ cm.

💡 C=2×22/7×7=44 cm
