Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Solve: 5x + 2 = 22

💡 5x=20; x=4

Q2. Solve: 3x − 7 = 14

💡 3x=21; x=7

Q3. Solve: x/4 + 2 = 5

💡 x/4=3; x=12

Q4. Solve: 2(x + 3) = 14

💡 2x+6=14; 2x=8; x=4

Q5. If 3x − 5 = x + 7, then x = ?

💡 3x−x=7+5; 2x=12; x=6

Q6. Solve: 10 − x = 3

💡 x = 10 − 3 = 7

Q7. The perimeter of a square is 36 cm. Side length = ?

💡 4s=36; s=9

Q8. Solve: 5(x − 2) = 15

💡 x−2=3; x=5

Q9. Solve: x/5 = 9

💡 x=9×5=45

Q10. Sheyvon earns Rs. x per hour. In 4 hours he earns Rs. 320. Solve for x.

💡 4x=320; x=80

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. x = −2 satisfies the equation x + 5 = 3.

💡 −2+5=3 ✓

Q12. Multiplying both sides of an equation by 0 gives a valid solution.

💡 Multiplying by 0 makes both sides 0, losing information.

Q13. 3(2x − 1) = 6x − 3

💡 3×2x=6x; 3×(−1)=−3. Correct.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Solve: 7x = 49. x = ___.

💡 49÷7=7

Q15. Solve: 2x + 9 = 17. x = ___.

💡 2x=8; x=4
