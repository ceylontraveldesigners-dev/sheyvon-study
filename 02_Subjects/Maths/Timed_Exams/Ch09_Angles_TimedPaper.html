Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Which is a reflex angle?

💡 200° is between 180° and 360° — reflex.

Q2. Find x if angles on a line are 2x and x.

💡 2x + x = 180; 3x = 180; x = 60.

Q3. Complement of 72° = ?

💡 90 − 72 = 18°.

Q4. Vertically opposite to 55° = ?

💡 Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Q5. Angles around a point: 90°, 90°, 120°, x. Find x.

💡 90+90+120+x=360; x=60°.

Q6. Two supplementary angles are in ratio 2:1. Find the smaller angle.

💡 2x+x=180; 3x=180; x=60°.

Q7. An acute angle is:

💡 Acute: 0° < angle < 90°.

Q8. If two angles are vertically opposite and one is 3y−10 and other is y+30, find y.

💡 3y−10=y+30; 2y=40; y=20.

Q9. Angles on a straight line: 3a, 2a, a. Find a.

💡 3a+2a+a=180; 6a=180; a=30.

Q10. Two complementary angles differ by 20°. The smaller is:

💡 x+(x+20)=90; 2x=70; x=35.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. The complement of 45° is also 45°.

💡 90°−45°=45°. They are equal.

Q12. An obtuse angle can be 91°.

💡 91° is between 90° and 180° — obtuse.

Q13. Angles at a point always add to 360°.

💡 A full revolution = 360°.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The supplement of 90° is ___°.

💡 180−90=90.

Q15. Vertically opposite to 143° is ___°.

💡 Vertically opposite angles are equal.
