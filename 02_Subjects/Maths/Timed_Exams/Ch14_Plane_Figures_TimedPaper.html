Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. An isosceles triangle has one angle of 40°. If it is the unique angle, what are the base angles?

💡 Base angles=(180−40)÷2=70°

Q2. A regular hexagon has side 5 cm. Perimeter = ?

💡 6×5=30 cm

Q3. Which is NOT a quadrilateral?

💡 Triangle has 3 sides — not a quadrilateral.

Q4. Angles of a quadrilateral: 3 are 90°. 4th angle = ?

💡 360−90−90−90=90°

Q5. A scalene triangle has:

💡 Scalene = all sides different.

Q6. Perimeter of a regular octagon with side 4 cm:

💡 8×4=32 cm

Q7. Triangle angles ratio 1:2:3. Largest angle = ?

💡 1x+2x+3x=180; x=30; largest=3×30=90°

Q8. A rhombus has:

💡 Rhombus: 4 equal sides, parallel opposite sides.

Q9. Which triangle has a 90° angle?

💡 Right-angled triangle has one 90° angle.

Q10. Perimeter of triangle with sides 5cm, 8cm, 11cm:

💡 5+8+11=24 cm

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. All equilateral triangles are also isosceles.

💡 Equilateral has 2+ equal sides, so it is also isosceles.

Q12. A rectangle has 4 axes of symmetry.

💡 A rectangle has 2 axes of symmetry.

Q13. The sum of angles in any quadrilateral is 360°.

💡 Always 360° for any quadrilateral.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

💡 4×9=36
