Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. A bottle holds 0.75 l. In ml = ?

💡 0.75×1000=750 ml

Q2. Total: 1.2 l + 400 ml = ? ml

💡 1200+400=1600 ml

Q3. Box 10cm×8cm×5cm. Capacity = ? ml

💡 V=400 cm³=400 ml

Q4. A tank holds 20 litres. 8500 ml removed. Remaining = ? litres

💡 20000−8500=11500 ml=11.5 l

Q5. 5 jugs each 600 ml. Total litres = ?

💡 5×600=3000ml=3l

Q6. Medicine: 5 ml per dose, 3 doses/day for 7 days. Total ml = ?

💡 5×3×7=105 ml

Q7. Half-fill a 6 l container: how many ml inside?

💡 6000÷2=3000 ml

Q8. 1.5 l + 250 ml + 750 ml = ? litres

💡 1500+250+750=2500 ml=2.5 l

Q9. How many 200 ml glasses from 5 litres?

💡 5000÷200=25

Q10. 3 litres 250 ml − 1 litre 700 ml = ?

💡 3250−1700=1550 ml=1 l 550 ml

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. 2.5 litres = 2500 ml

💡 2.5×1000=2500 ml

Q12. Capacity and volume are completely unrelated.

💡 1 cm³ = 1 ml; they are directly related.

Q13. A container with volume 1000 cm³ holds exactly 1 litre.

💡 1000 cm³ = 1000 ml = 1 litre.
