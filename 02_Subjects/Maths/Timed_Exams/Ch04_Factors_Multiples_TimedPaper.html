Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. How many factors does 18 have?

💡 Factors: 1,2,3,6,9,18 = 6 factors.

Q2. Which pair of numbers has HCF = 5?

💡 Factors of 10: 1,2,5,10. Factors of 15: 1,3,5,15. HCF=5.

Q3. LCM(3, 4, 6) = ?

💡 Multiples of 6: 6,12. 12 is also a multiple of 3 and 4. LCM=12.

Q4. Prime factorisation of 36 = ?

💡 36 = 4×9 = 2²×3².

Q5. Which is a prime number?

💡 41 has no factors other than 1 and 41.

Q6. The 5th multiple of 9 is:

💡 9×5=45.

Q7. HCF(24, 36) = ?

💡 Common factors: 1,2,3,4,6,12. HCF=12.

Q8. How many prime numbers are there between 10 and 20?

💡 11,13,17,19 — four primes.

Q9. The smallest prime number is:

💡 2 is the smallest prime (and only even prime).

Q10. A number that is both a factor of 12 and a multiple of 4 is:

💡 4 is a factor of 12 (12÷4=3) and 4=4×1.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. The HCF of two different prime numbers is always 1.

💡 Primes only share 1 as a common factor.

Q12. Every multiple of 6 is also a multiple of 3.

💡 6=2×3, so all multiples of 6 contain factor 3.

Q13. The LCM of two numbers is always larger than both numbers.

💡 LCM(5,5)=5, not larger.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The HCF of 20 and 30 is ___.
