Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. What is 11:45 pm in 24-hour time?

💡 11+12=23. So 23:45.

Q2. A train departs at 06:50 and arrives at 09:20. Journey time?

💡 09:20−06:50 = 2h 30min.

Q3. How many hours in a week?

💡 7 × 24 = 168 hours.

Q4. Convert 150 minutes to hours and minutes.

💡 150÷60=2 remainder 30. So 2h 30min.

Q5. What is 00:00 in 12-hour time?

💡 00:00 = 12:00 am (midnight).

Q6. A show runs from 20:15 to 22:45. Duration?

💡 22:45−20:15 = 2h 30min.

Q7. How many days in 4 weeks?

💡 4 × 7 = 28 days.

Q8. Convert 2 h 15 min to minutes.

💡 2×60+15=135.

Q9. 3:20 am in 24-hour time is:

💡 am times stay the same in 24h (with leading zero).

Q10. From 9:50 am to 12:20 pm is:

💡 09:50 to 12:20 = 2h 30min.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. 60 seconds = 1 minute and 60 minutes = 1 hour.

💡 Standard time conversions.

Q12. 14:30 in 12-hour time is 2:30 am.

💡 14:30 = 2:30 pm (not am).

Q13. A leap year occurs every 4 years.

💡 Leap years: 2020, 2024, 2028...

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. 5 hours = ___ minutes.

💡 5×60=300.

Q15. 18:00 in 12-hour time is ___ pm.

💡 18−12=6. So 6:00 pm.
