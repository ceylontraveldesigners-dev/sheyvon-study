Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Interior angle of regular octagon?

💡 (8−2)×180÷8=135°

Q2. How many squares meet at one vertex in square tessellation?

💡 90×4=360

Q3. Interior angle of equilateral triangle?

💡 (3−2)×180÷3=60°

Q4. Which two shapes tile together: triangle+square?

💡 Triangles and squares can form semi-regular tessellations.

Q5. Interior angle of regular decagon (10 sides)?

💡 (10−2)×180÷10=144°

Q6. Can rectangles tessellate?

💡 Any rectangle can tessellate.

Q7. Why can't regular pentagons tessellate?

💡 360÷108 is not a whole number.

Q8. How many regular hexagons meet at one vertex?

💡 120×3=360

Q9. Interior angle of regular polygon with 12 sides?

💡 (12−2)×180÷12=150°

Q10. Which shape CAN tessellate on its own?

💡 A rhombus (like a parallelogram) can tessellate.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. Only regular polygons can tessellate.

💡 Irregular shapes like scalene triangles and rectangles also tessellate.

Q12. Three shapes meeting at a vertex must have angles adding to 360°.

💡 That is the condition for no gaps.

Q13. A regular hexagon has an interior angle of 120°.

💡 (6−2)×180÷6=120°

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The three regular polygons that tessellate alone are triangle, square and ___.

💡 Equilateral triangle, square, regular hexagon.

Q15. Interior angle formula for n-sided regular polygon: (n−2) × 180 ÷ ___.

💡 Divide by n.
