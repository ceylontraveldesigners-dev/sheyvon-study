Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Cuboid: 7m × 5m × 3m. Volume = ?

💡 7×5×3=105 m³

Q2. How many 1cm³ cubes fill a box 4×3×5 cm?

💡 4×3×5=60

Q3. Cube volume = 216 cm³. Side = ?

💡 ∛216=6 cm

Q4. V=360 cm³, l=12cm, h=5cm. Find w.

💡 w=360÷(12×5)=6 cm

Q5. A water tank: 2m × 1.5m × 0.8m. Volume in m³ = ?

💡 2×1.5×0.8=2.4 m³

Q6. Which has largest volume: 3×3×3, 2×4×4, or 2×2×8?

💡 Actually 2×4×4=32 and 2×2×8=32 are largest. Let me recalc: 27, 32, 32. So 2×4×4 or 2×2×8.

Q7. Tank fills at 30 cm³/min. How long to fill 600 cm³?

💡 600÷30=20 min

Q8. Cuboid has square base side 4cm and height 9cm. Volume = ?

💡 4×4×9=144 cm³

Q9. 1 litre = 1000 cm³. Volume of 2.5 litre container = ?

💡 2.5×1000=2500 cm³

Q10. Double length only of 3×4×5 cuboid. New volume = ?

💡 6×4×5=120 cm³

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. Doubling all three dimensions multiplies volume by 8.

💡 V→(2l)(2w)(2h)=8lwh

Q12. 1 m³ = 1000 cm³

💡 1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³

Q13. Volume of a cube = side³

💡 V = s × s × s = s³

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Cuboid 8cm × 5cm × 4cm. Volume = ___ cm³.

💡 8×5×4=160

Q15. Cube with side 10cm. Volume = ___ cm³.

💡 10³=1000
