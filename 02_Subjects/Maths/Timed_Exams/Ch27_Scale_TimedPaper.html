Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Scale 1:500. Map = 4.5cm. Real = ?

💡 4.5×500=2250cm=22.5m

Q2. Scale 1:2000. Real = 3km. Map = ?

💡 3km=300000cm. 300000÷2000=150cm? Wait: 300000÷2000=150cm=1.5m. Hmm, let me recalc: 3km=3000m=300000cm. ÷2000=150cm=1.5m. So 150cm.

Q3. A blueprint scale is 1:50. Room shown as 6cm×4cm. Real size?

💡 6×50=300cm=3m; 4×50=200cm=2m

Q4. Scale factor 2:1 means the drawing is:

💡 2:1 = 2 units drawn for 1 real unit.

Q5. Map scale 1:25000. Two towns 8cm apart on map. Real distance?

💡 8×25000=200000cm=2000m=2km

Q6. Scale 1:300. Real = 900m. Map = ?

💡 90000cm÷300=300cm

Q7. Which scale is most detailed (largest scale)?

💡 1:100 is largest (most detail); smaller denominator = more detail.

Q8. Sheyvon draws a garden 1:40. Path on drawing = 7.5cm. Real length?

💡 7.5×40=300cm=3m

Q9. A model car scale 1:24. Real car 360cm long. Model length?

💡 360÷24=15cm

Q10. Scale 1:n. Map=5cm, Real=250m. Find n.

💡 250m=25000cm. n=25000÷5=5000

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. Scale 1:1 means the drawing is the same size as reality.

💡 1:1 = actual size.

Q12. On a 1:100 scale, 1m in real life = 100cm on the drawing.

💡 1m real = 100cm real = 1cm on drawing.

Q13. A larger scale number (denominator) gives more detail.

💡 Smaller denominator = more detail (1:100 more detailed than 1:10000).

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Scale 1:200. Real = 10m. Map length = ___ cm.

💡 10m=1000cm. 1000÷200=5cm

Q15. Scale 1:50. Map = 8cm. Real = ___ cm.

💡 8×50=400cm
