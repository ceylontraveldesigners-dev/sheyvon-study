Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Point A = (2,3), Point B = (8,3). Midpoint?

💡 x: (2+8)/2=5. y: (3+3)/2=3.

Q2. Which point lies on the y-axis?

💡 On y-axis: x=0.

Q3. Point (−2,−7) is in which quadrant?

💡 Both negative → Q3.

Q4. Which point is 5 units from the origin along the x-axis?

💡 On x-axis: y=0. So (5,0).

Q5. If (a,4) is in Q2, then a is:

💡 Q2: x negative.

Q6. Translate point (3,2) by moving 4 right and 3 down. New point?

💡 3+4=7; 2−3=−1. (7,−1)

Q7. A square has vertices (1,1),(4,1),(4,4),(1,4). Its centre?

💡 x: (1+4)/2=2.5. y: (1+4)/2=2.5.

Q8. Distance between (0,0) and (3,4)?

💡 √(3²+4²)=√25=5

Q9. Reflect (4,−3) in the x-axis.

💡 Reflecting in x-axis: y changes sign. (4,3)

Q10. Point P = (−5, 0) lies on which axis?

💡 y=0 means on x-axis.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. (3,5) and (5,3) are the same point.

💡 Order matters: (x,y). Different points.

Q12. All points on the x-axis have y=0.

💡 The x-axis is where y=0.

Q13. Q1 contains points with both coordinates positive.

💡 Q1: (+,+)

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The coordinates of the origin are ___ .

💡 Origin = (0,0)

Q15. Point (−3, 7) is in Quadrant ___.

💡 Negative x, positive y → Q2.
