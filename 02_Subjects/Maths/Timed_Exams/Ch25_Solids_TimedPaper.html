Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. A cuboid has how many edges?

💡 12 edges (same as cube).

Q2. Square pyramid: F=5, V=5. How many edges?

💡 F+V−E=2; 10−E=2; E=8

Q3. Which solid has 2 circular faces and 1 curved face?

💡 Cylinder: 2 circles + 1 curved surface.

Q4. Which of these is a polyhedron?

💡 Polyhedra have flat polygonal faces.

Q5. A hexagonal prism has how many faces?

💡 2 hexagons + 6 rectangles = 8 faces.

Q6. The net of a cylinder includes:

💡 Top + bottom circles + unrolled rectangle.

Q7. Pentagonal pyramid: 5 triangles + 1 pentagon. Vertices = ?

💡 5 base + 1 apex = 6 vertices.

Q8. F+V−E=2 for a pentagonal prism. If F=7, V=10, find E.

💡 7+10−E=2; E=15

Q9. Which solid has only triangular faces?

💡 A tetrahedron has 4 triangular faces.

Q10. How many faces does a hexagonal pyramid have?

💡 6 triangles + 1 hexagon = 7 faces.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. A triangular prism has 5 faces.

💡 2 triangles + 3 rectangles = 5 faces.

Q12. All prisms have rectangular side faces.

💡 Side faces of a prism are always rectangles.

Q13. A cone has 2 faces.

💡 1 circular base + 1 curved surface = 2 faces.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. A cube has ___ edges.

💡 12 edges.

Q15. Euler's formula: F + V − E = ___.

💡 Always equals 2.
