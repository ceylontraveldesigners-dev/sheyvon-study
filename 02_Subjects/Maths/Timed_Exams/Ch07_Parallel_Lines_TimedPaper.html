Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. What shape do corresponding angles make?

💡 Corresponding angles form an F-shape.

Q2. Two parallel lines cut by transversal. Alternate angle = 55°. Other alternate angle = ?

💡 Alternate angles are equal.

Q3. If co-interior angles are equal, each must be:

💡 Equal co-interior angles: 180°÷2=90°.

Q4. Which angle type uses the C-shape?

💡 C or U shape = co-interior angles.

Q5. Corresponding angle = 3x and its pair = 90°. Find x.

💡 3x=90, x=30.

Q6. Alternate angle = 2y and its pair = 70°. Find y.

💡 2y=70, y=35.

Q7. Which statement about parallel lines is correct?

💡 Parallel lines never meet.

Q8. Co-interior angle = 4a and 5a. Find a.

💡 4a+5a=180; 9a=180; a=20.

Q9. Corresponding angles apply when:

💡 These angle rules only apply to parallel lines.

Q10. Alternate angle = 38°. Its co-interior partner on the same side = ?

💡 Co-interior: 180°−38°=142°.

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. Alternate angles and corresponding angles are both equal.

💡 Both types are equal when lines are parallel.

Q12. Co-interior angles can add to 90° if lines are steep enough.

💡 Co-interior angles always add to exactly 180°.

Q13. A transversal must be perpendicular to the parallel lines.

💡 A transversal can be at any angle.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Alternate angles form a ___ shape.

💡 Z-shape.

Q15. If corresponding angles are equal, the lines must be ___.

💡 Equal corresponding angles → parallel lines.
