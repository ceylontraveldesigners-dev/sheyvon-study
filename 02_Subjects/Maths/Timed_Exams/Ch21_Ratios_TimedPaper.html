Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Simplify 36:48.

💡 HCF=12. 3:4

Q2. A:B=2:5. If A=14, find B.

💡 14÷2=7. B=5×7=35

Q3. Divide 120 in ratio 3:5.

💡 120÷8=15. 3×15=45, 5×15=75

Q4. Total students 42, ratio girls:boys=4:3. How many girls?

💡 42÷7=6. 4×6=24

Q5. Are 3:7 and 9:21 equivalent?

💡 9:21→3:7. Yes.

Q6. Simplify 100:75.

💡 HCF=25. 4:3

Q7. Mix cement:sand=1:4. For 25 kg total, how much cement?

💡 25÷5=5. 1×5=5 kg

Q8. What fraction is the smaller share when dividing in ratio 2:3?

💡 Smaller share = 2/(2+3)=2/5

Q9. Ratio of apples to oranges = 5:2. Total fruit = 35. Oranges = ?

💡 35÷7=5. 2×5=10

Q10. Scale model ratio 1:50. Real length 300 cm. Model length = ?

💡 300÷50=6 cm

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. Ratio 1:1 means the two quantities are equal.

💡 1:1 → equal shares.

Q12. Equivalent ratios must have the same numbers.

💡 4:6 and 2:3 are equivalent but have different numbers.

Q13. To divide Rs.100 in ratio 3:7, each share is Rs.30 and Rs.70.

💡 100÷10=10. 3×10=30, 7×10=70.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. Simplify 45:60 = ___:4.

💡 HCF=15. 45:60=3:4

Q15. Divide 90 in ratio 2:1. Larger share = ___.

💡 90÷3=30. 2×30=60
