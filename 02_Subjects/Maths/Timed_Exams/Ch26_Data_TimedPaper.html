Section A — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1. Data: 3,6,2,8,1. Mean = ?

💡 (3+6+2+8+1)÷5=20÷5=4

Q2. Data: 4,7,4,9,4,6. Mode = ?

💡 4 appears 3 times.

Q3. Data: 5,8,3,10,6. Median = ?

💡 Sorted:3,5,6,8,10. Middle=6.

Q4. Five test scores: 65,72,80,55,78. Mean = ?

💡 (65+72+80+55+78)÷5=350÷5=70

Q5. Data: 2,4,6,8. Median = ?

💡 Even: (4+6)÷2=5

Q6. Range of 100,45,73,28,62 = ?

💡 100−28=72

Q7. If mean of 4 numbers is 9, their sum = ?

💡 Mean×count=9×4=36

Q8. Which average is most affected by extreme values?

💡 Mean uses all values, so outliers affect it most.

Q9. Data: 10,10,10,10. What is the mode, median and mean?

💡 All three equal 10.

Q10. Add value 20 to set {5,10,15}. New mean = ?

💡 (5+10+15+20)÷4=50÷4=12.5

Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. A data set can have more than one mode.

💡 e.g. 2,3,3,5,5 has two modes: 3 and 5.

Q12. The median is always a value in the data set.

💡 With even count, median can be the average of two values.

Q13. Mean, median and mode are all measures of central tendency.

💡 All three represent the 'centre' of data.

Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 marks)

Q14. The range of {15, 27, 8, 33, 19} = ___.

💡 33−8=25

Q15. Mean of {x, 2x, 3x} when x=4 is ___.

💡 4+8+12=24; 24÷3=8
