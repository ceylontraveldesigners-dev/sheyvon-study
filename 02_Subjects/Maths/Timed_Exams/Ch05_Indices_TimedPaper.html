Section B — True or False (3 marks)

Q11. 3² × 3³ = 9⁵

💡 3² × 3³ = 3⁵, not 9⁵. The base stays the same.

Q12. 2⁸ ÷ 2⁸ = 1

💡 2⁸⁻⁸ = 2⁰ = 1.

Q13. The index tells us how many times the base is multiplied by itself.

💡 3⁴ means 3×3×3×3.
