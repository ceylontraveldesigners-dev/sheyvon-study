📝 Section A — Multiple Choice

Q1. What does a perpendicular bisector do?

💡 Perpendicular bisector = 90° at midpoint.

Q2. To construct an angle bisector, you need:

💡 Construction uses compass and ruler.

Q3. An equilateral triangle is constructed with sides of 6cm. All sides are:

💡 All 3 sides = 6 cm.

Q4. What does the locus of points equidistant from two points form?

💡 Equidistant from two points → perpendicular bisector.

Q5. When constructing, which tool measures length?

💡 Ruler measures length; compass transfers length.

✅ Section B — True or False

Q6. A perpendicular bisector passes through the midpoint of a line segment.

💡 It bisects (halves) the segment at 90°.

Q7. An angle bisector divides an angle into three equal parts.

💡 It divides into TWO equal parts.

Q8. Geometric construction uses a compass and ruler.

💡 These are the standard tools.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank

Q9. The perpendicular bisector meets the original line at ___°.

💡 Perpendicular = 90°.

Q10. Bisect means to divide into ___ equal parts.

💡 Bisect = divide into 2 equal parts.
