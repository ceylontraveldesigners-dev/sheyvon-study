No time limit | 10 questions

📝 Section A — Multiple Choice

Q1. How many faces does a cube have?

💡 A cube has 6 square faces.

Q2. Euler's formula is:

💡 F+V−E=2 for any polyhedron.

Q3. A triangular prism has how many edges?

💡 3 on each triangle + 3 connecting = 9 edges.

Q4. Which solid has no edges or vertices?

💡 A sphere has no edges or flat faces.

Q5. The net of a cube has how many squares?

💡 6 square faces unfold to make the net.

✅ Section B — True or False

Q6. A sphere has 1 face, 0 edges, 0 vertices.

💡 Correct — the curved surface counts as 1 face.

Q7. A square pyramid has 4 triangular faces and 1 square base.

💡 Total = 5 faces.

Q8. Euler's formula applies to all 3D shapes.

💡 It applies to polyhedra (flat-faced solids), not curved solids like spheres.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank

Q9. A cube has ___ vertices.

💡 8 corner points.

Q10. F=5, V=5, E=___. (Use Euler's formula)

💡 F+V−E=2; 5+5−E=2; E=8
