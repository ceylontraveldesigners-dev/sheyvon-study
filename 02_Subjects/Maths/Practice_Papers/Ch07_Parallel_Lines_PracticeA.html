📝 Section A — Multiple Choice (5 questions)

Q1. Corresponding angles between parallel lines are:

💡 Corresponding angles are always equal.

Q2. Alternate angles form which letter shape?

💡 Alternate angles form a Z-shape.

Q3. If one co-interior angle is 75°, the other is:

💡 Co-interior: 180° − 75° = 105°.

Q4. Parallel lines are marked with:

💡 Arrow marks (→) indicate parallel lines.

Q5. A transversal is:

💡 A transversal cuts across parallel lines.

✅ Section B — True or False (3 questions)

Q6. Alternate angles are equal when lines are parallel.

💡 Alternate (Z) angles are always equal.

Q7. Co-interior angles add to 90°.

💡 Co-interior angles add to 180°, not 90°.

Q8. Parallel lines eventually meet.

💡 Parallel lines never meet.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 questions)

Q9. If a corresponding angle is 72°, the matching angle is ___°.

💡 Corresponding angles are equal.

Q10. Co-interior angles always add up to ___°.

💡 Co-interior angles are supplementary.
