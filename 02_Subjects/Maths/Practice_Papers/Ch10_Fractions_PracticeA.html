📝 Section A — Multiple Choice (5 questions)

Q1. Which is a proper fraction?

💡 Proper fraction: numerator < denominator. ³⁄₇ qualifies.

Q2. Simplify ¹⁵⁄₂₅.

💡 HCF(15,25)=5. 15÷5=3, 25÷5=5. Answer: ³⁄₅.

Q3. ¹⁄₄ + ¹⁄₂ = ?

💡 LCD=4. ¹⁄₄ + ²⁄₄ = ³⁄₄.

Q4. ³⁄₅ × ¹⁰⁄₉ = ?

💡 30÷45 = ²⁄₃.

Q5. Convert 2²⁄₃ to an improper fraction.

💡 2×3+2=8. So ⁸⁄₃.

✅ Section B — True or False (3 questions)

Q6. ¹⁄₂ + ¹⁄₃ = ²⁄₅

💡 You cannot add numerators and denominators. ¹⁄₂ + ¹⁄₃ = ⁵⁄₆.

Q7. ½ = 0.5

💡 1 divided by 2 = 0.5.

Q8. To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal.

💡 Dividing by ²⁄₃ means multiplying by ³⁄₂.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 questions)

Q9. The reciprocal of ³⁄₄ is ___.

💡 Flip the fraction: ⁴⁄₃.

Q10. ¹²⁄₁₆ simplified is ___.

💡 HCF(12,16)=4. 12÷4=3, 16÷4=4.
