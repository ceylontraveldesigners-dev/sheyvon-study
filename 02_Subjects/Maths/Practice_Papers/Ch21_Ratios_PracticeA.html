No time limit | 10 questions

📝 Section A — Multiple Choice

Q1. Simplify 15:25.

💡 HCF=5. 15÷5=3, 25÷5=5.

Q2. Divide 56 in ratio 3:4.

💡 Sum=7. 56÷7=8. 3×8=24, 4×8=32

Q3. Are 2:5 and 4:10 equivalent?

💡 4:10→2:5. Yes.

Q4. A class has 18 boys and 12 girls. Ratio boys:girls?

💡 HCF=6. 18:12→3:2

Q5. If ratio of red:blue=5:3 and there are 40 red, how many blue?

💡 40÷5=8. Blue=3×8=24

✅ Section B — True or False

Q6. Ratio 4:6 simplified is 2:3.

💡 HCF=2. 4:6→2:3.

Q7. Dividing 90 in ratio 1:2 gives 30 and 60.

💡 90÷3=30. 1×30=30, 2×30=60.

Q8. 3:4 means 3/4.

💡 3:4 means 3 out of 7 (3/7) and 4 out of 7 (4/7).

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank

Q9. Simplify 20:30 = ___:3.

💡 20:30→2:3

Q10. Divide 72 in ratio 1:3. The larger share = ___.

💡 72÷4=18. 3×18=54
