🗺️ Chapter 27 — Scale Drawings — Practice Set A

No time limit | 10 questions

📝 Section A — Multiple Choice

Q1. Scale 1:100. Map length 6cm. Real length?

💡 6×100=600cm=6m

Q2. Scale 1:500. Real length 25m. Map length?

💡 2500cm÷500=5cm

Q3. Scale 1:50000. 3cm on map = how many km?

💡 3×50000=150000cm=1500m=1.5km

Q4. Which scale makes a drawing LARGER than real life?

💡 10:1 means 10 units drawn for every 1 real unit.

Q5. A room is 5m × 4m. Scale 1:50. Drawing dimensions?

💡 500÷50=10cm; 400÷50=8cm

✅ Section B — True or False

Q6. Scale 1:200 means 1cm on drawing = 200cm in reality.

💡 That is the definition.

Q7. The same scale must be used for length and width on a drawing.

💡 All dimensions use the same scale.

Q8. A scale drawing must always be smaller than real life.

💡 An enlarged drawing (like a cell under microscope) is bigger.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank

Q9. Scale 1:400. Map = 3cm. Real = ___ m.

💡 3×400=1200cm=12m

Q10. Scale 1:250. Real = 500m. Map = ___ cm.

💡 50000cm÷250=200cm
