📝 Section A — Multiple Choice (5 questions)

Q1. What is 3 + 4 × 2?

💡 Multiply first: 3 + 8 = 11.

Q2. What is (6 + 2) × 3?

💡 Brackets first: 8 × 3 = 24.

Q3. What does BODMAS stand for?

💡 BODMAS = Brackets, Orders, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

Q4. What is 18 ÷ 3 × 2?

💡 Left to right: 18÷3=6, then 6×2=12.

Q5. What is 10 − 2 × 3 + 1?

💡 Multiply first: 10 − 6 + 1 = 5.

✅ Section B — True or False (3 questions)

Q6. In 5 + 2 × 3, you add first.

Q7. (4 + 6) ÷ 2 = 5

💡 Brackets: 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Correct.

Q8. Division by zero is defined.

💡 Division by zero is undefined.

✏️ Section C — Fill in the Blank (2 questions)

💡 2+5=7, then 3×7=21.
