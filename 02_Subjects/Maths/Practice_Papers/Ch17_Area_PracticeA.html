📝 Section A — Multiple Choice

Q1. Area of a rectangle 9 cm by 4 cm:

💡 9×4=36 cm²

Q2. Area of a triangle: base=6m, height=5m:

💡 ½×6×5=15 m²

Q3. A square has area 64 cm². Its side length is:

💡 √64=8 cm

Q4. Area of parallelogram: base=10cm, height=7cm:

💡 10×7=70 cm²

Q5. Which unit is used for area?

💡 Area is measured in square units.

✅ Section B — True or False

Q6. Area and perimeter have the same units.

💡 Area: square units (cm²); Perimeter: length units (cm).

Q7. Area of triangle = ½ × base × height.

💡 Correct formula.

Q8. A 4cm × 4cm square has the same area as a 2cm × 8cm rectangle.

💡 16 cm² = 16 cm²

Q9. Area of a square with side 7 cm = ___ cm².

💡 7²=49 cm²

Q10. Area of triangle with base 12m and height 5m = ___ m².
