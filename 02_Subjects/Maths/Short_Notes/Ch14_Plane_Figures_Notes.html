🔑 Key Concepts — Plane Figures

Plane Figure: A flat, two-dimensional shape. Examples: triangle, square, rectangle, circle.
Triangle Types by sides: Equilateral (3 equal sides), Isosceles (2 equal sides), Scalene (no equal sides).
Triangle Types by angles: Acute (all angles < 90°), Right (one 90° angle), Obtuse (one angle > 90°).
Quadrilaterals: 4-sided polygons. Square, Rectangle, Parallelogram, Rhombus, Trapezium, Kite.
Perimeter: Total distance around the outside of a shape. Sum of all sides.
Angle sum of triangle: The three interior angles of any triangle always add up to 180°.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Angles in a triangle always add to 180°. Angles in a quadrilateral always add to 360°.
✅ Rule 2: A square is a special rectangle AND a special rhombus.
❌ Common Mistake: All squares are rectangles, but NOT all rectangles are squares.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Triangle angles: 70°, 55°, x. Find x.

→ 180 − 70 − 55 = 55°
Example 2: Perimeter of rectangle: length=8cm, width=5cm

→ P = 2(8+5) = 2(13) = 26 cm
Example 3: A regular pentagon with side 6 cm. Perimeter?

→ 5 × 6 = 30 cm
