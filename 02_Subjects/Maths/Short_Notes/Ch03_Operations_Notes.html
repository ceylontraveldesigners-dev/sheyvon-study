🔑 Key Concepts — Operations

BODMAS Rule: The order to perform operations: Brackets, Orders (powers/roots), Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.
Addition: Combining two or more numbers to get a total (sum).
Subtraction: Finding the difference between two numbers.
Multiplication: Repeated addition. a × b = b × a (commutative).
Division: Splitting into equal groups. a ÷ b = a/b. Division by zero is undefined.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Always work inside Brackets first, then Orders (powers), then × and ÷ left to right, then + and − left to right.
✅ Rule 2: If only × and ÷ appear together, work left to right. Same for + and −.
❌ Common Mistake: Don't add before multiplying! 2 + 3 × 4 = 2 + 12 = 14, NOT 5 × 4 = 20.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Calculate 3 + 4 × 2

→ Multiply first: 3 + 8 = 11
Example 2: Calculate (5 + 3) × 2 − 4

→ Brackets first: 8 × 2 − 4 = 16 − 4 = 12
Example 3: Calculate 20 ÷ 4 + 3 × 2

→ Div and Mult first: 5 + 6 = 11
