🔑 Key Concepts — Capacity

Capacity: The amount of liquid a container can hold. Measured in millilitres (ml) and litres (l).
Conversions: 1 litre = 1000 ml. 1 ml = 1 cm³.
Reading Scales: Find the value each small division represents, then count divisions.
Practical use: Cooking, filling tanks, medicine doses, fuel.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: 1 litre = 1000 ml. To convert litres to ml: multiply by 1000. ml to litres: divide by 1000.
✅ Rule 2: 1 cm³ = 1 ml. So volume of a cuboid in cm³ = its capacity in ml.
❌ Common Mistake: Don't confuse volume (cm³) and capacity (ml/litres) — they are related but not the same word.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Convert 3.5 litres to ml.

→ 3.5 × 1000 = 3500 ml
Example 2: Convert 750 ml to litres.

→ 750 ÷ 1000 = 0.75 litres
Example 3: A tank 20cm × 15cm × 10cm. Capacity in litres.

→ V = 3000 cm³ = 3000 ml = 3 litres
