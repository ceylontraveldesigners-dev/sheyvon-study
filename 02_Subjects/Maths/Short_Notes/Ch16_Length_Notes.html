🔑 Key Concepts — Length

Units of Length: Millimetres (mm), Centimetres (cm), Metres (m), Kilometres (km).
Conversions: 10 mm = 1 cm. 100 cm = 1 m. 1000 m = 1 km.
Perimeter: The total length of the boundary of a shape. Add all sides.
Practical Uses: Maps, scale drawings, measuring distances, construction.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Larger unit → smaller unit: MULTIPLY. Smaller → larger: DIVIDE.
✅ Rule 2: Always use the same units when adding lengths.
❌ Common Mistake: 100 cm = 1 m (NOT 10 cm = 1 m). 1000 m = 1 km (NOT 100 m).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Convert 3.5 km to metres.

→ 3.5 × 1000 = 3500 m
Example 2: Convert 450 cm to metres.

→ 450 ÷ 100 = 4.5 m
Example 3: Perimeter of rectangle: 8.5 m × 3.2 m.

→ 2(8.5 + 3.2) = 2(11.7) = 23.4 m
