🔑 Key Concepts — Angles

Angle Types: Acute: 0°–90°. Right: exactly 90°. Obtuse: 90°–180°. Straight: 180°. Reflex: 180°–360°. Full turn: 360°.
Complementary Angles: Two angles that add to 90°. e.g. 35° and 55° are complementary.
Supplementary Angles: Two angles that add to 180°. e.g. 70° and 110° are supplementary.
Angles at a Point: All angles around a single point add up to 360°.
Vertically Opposite Angles: When two lines cross, the opposite angles formed are equal.
Angles on a Straight Line: Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Complementary → adds to 90°. Supplementary → adds to 180°. Full turn → 360°.
✅ Rule 2: Vertically opposite angles are ALWAYS equal.
❌ Common Mistake: Don't confuse complementary (90°) and supplementary (180°). Remember: 'C' comes before 'S' and 90 < 180.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Find the complement of 34°.

→ 90° − 34° = 56°
Example 2: Find the supplement of 110°.

→ 180° − 110° = 70°
Example 3: Angles at a point: 90°, 120°, x°. Find x.

→ 90 + 120 + x = 360 → x = 150°
