🔑 Key Concepts — Solids

Solid: A 3D shape with length, width, and height. e.g. cube, cuboid, sphere, cone, cylinder, pyramid.
Faces: Flat surfaces on a solid. A cube has 6 faces.
Edges: Lines where two faces meet. A cube has 12 edges.
Vertices: Corner points where edges meet. A cube has 8 vertices.
Euler's Formula: F + V − E = 2, where F=Faces, V=Vertices, E=Edges.
Net: A flat 2D shape that folds into a 3D solid.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Euler's Formula: F + V − E = 2. Always check this for any polyhedron.
✅ Rule 2: Sphere has 0 edges, 0 vertices, 1 curved face. Cylinder has 3 faces (2 circles + 1 curved), 2 edges, 0 vertices.
❌ Common Mistake: Don't confuse edges (lines) and faces (flat surfaces). A cube has 6 faces but 12 edges.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Cube: F=6, V=8, E=12. Euler check?

→ 6+8−12=2 ✓
Example 2: Triangular prism: F, V, E?

→ F=5 (2 triangles+3 rectangles), V=6, E=9. 5+6−9=2 ✓
Example 3: What solid has a circular base and comes to a point?

→ A cone.
