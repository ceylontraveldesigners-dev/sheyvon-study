🔑 Key Concepts — Area

Area: The amount of space inside a 2D shape. Measured in square units (cm², m², km²).
Rectangle: Area = length × width
Square: Area = side × side = side²
Triangle: Area = ½ × base × height
Parallelogram: Area = base × height (perpendicular height, not slanted side)
Composite Shapes: Break into simpler shapes, calculate each area, then add or subtract.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Area of triangle = ½ × base × height. The HEIGHT must be PERPENDICULAR to the base.
✅ Rule 2: Area is always in SQUARE units (cm², m²).
❌ Common Mistake: Confusing perimeter (length around) with area (space inside).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Area of rectangle: 8 cm × 5 cm

→ A = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²
Example 2: Area of triangle: base=10cm, height=6cm

→ A = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm²
Example 3: Area of parallelogram: base=9cm, height=4cm

→ A = 9 × 4 = 36 cm²
