🔑 Key Concepts — Percentages

Percentage: A fraction with denominator 100. 45% = 45/100 = 0.45
Fraction to %: Multiply by 100. 3/4 × 100 = 75%
% to Fraction: Divide by 100. 60% = 60/100 = 3/5
% of a Quantity: Percentage ÷ 100 × total. 20% of 80 = 0.2 × 80 = 16
% Increase: Increase ÷ original × 100
% Decrease: Decrease ÷ original × 100

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: To find X% of a value: divide by 100, then multiply by X.
✅ Rule 2: % change = (change ÷ original) × 100.
❌ Common Mistake: 50% of 80 ≠ 80 ÷ 50. It is 80 × 50/100 = 40.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Find 35% of 200

→ 0.35 × 200 = 70
Example 2: Convert 3/8 to a percentage

→ 3/8 × 100 = 37.5%
Example 3: Price rises from Rs.400 to Rs.480. % increase?

→ Increase=80. 80÷400×100=20%
