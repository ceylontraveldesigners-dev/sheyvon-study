🔑 Key Concepts — Time

Units of Time: Seconds (s), Minutes (min), Hours (h), Days, Weeks, Months, Years. 60 s = 1 min; 60 min = 1 h; 24 h = 1 day; 7 days = 1 week; 12 months = 1 year.
12-Hour Clock: Uses am (midnight to noon) and pm (noon to midnight). Goes from 12:00 am to 11:59 pm.
24-Hour Clock: Uses 00:00 to 23:59. No am/pm needed. 1:00 pm = 13:00; 9:00 pm = 21:00.
Converting 12h to 24h: For pm times (except 12 pm), add 12 hours. 3:30 pm → 15:30. 12:00 am = 00:00.
Duration: Time elapsed = End time − Start time. Convert units carefully.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: 12:00 noon = 12:00 (24h) = 12:00 pm. Midnight = 00:00 (24h) = 12:00 am.
✅ Rule 2: When calculating duration, convert to minutes or seconds to avoid errors.
❌ Common Mistake: 12:00 pm is NOON, NOT midnight. 12:00 am is midnight.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Convert 3:45 pm to 24-hour time.

→ 3:45 pm → 3 + 12 = 15:45
Example 2: A film starts at 14:20 and lasts 1 h 45 min. When does it end?

→ 14:20 + 1:45 = 16:05 (4:05 pm)
Example 3: Convert 09:00 (24h) to 12-hour time.

→ 09:00 = 9:00 am
