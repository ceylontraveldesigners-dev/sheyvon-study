🔑 Key Concepts — Directed Numbers

Directed Numbers: Numbers with a positive (+) or negative (−) sign. They can be shown on a number line.
Positive Numbers: Numbers greater than zero. Located to the right of 0 on the number line.
Negative Numbers: Numbers less than zero. Located to the left of 0 on the number line.
Adding Directed Numbers: Adding a negative is the same as subtracting. (+5) + (−3) = 5 − 3 = 2.
Subtracting Directed Numbers: Subtracting a negative is the same as adding. 5 − (−3) = 5 + 3 = 8.
Multiplying/Dividing: Same signs → positive result. Different signs → negative result. (+) × (−) = (−). (−) × (−) = (+).

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Two negatives next to each other become a positive: −(−3) = +3.
✅ Rule 2: On the number line, moving right = increasing, moving left = decreasing.
❌ Common Mistake: (−2) × (−3) is NOT −6. It is +6. Negative × Negative = Positive.

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: (+7) + (−4)

→ = 7 − 4 = 3
Example 2: (−3) − (−5)

→ = −3 + 5 = 2
Example 3: (−4) × (−6)

→ = +24 (negative × negative = positive)
