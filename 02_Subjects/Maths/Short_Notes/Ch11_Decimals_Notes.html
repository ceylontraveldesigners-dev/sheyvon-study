🔑 Key Concepts — Decimals

Decimal: A number with a decimal point separating the whole part from the fractional part. e.g. 3.75
Place Value: Tenths (0.1), Hundredths (0.01), Thousandths (0.001). In 4.375: 3 is tenths, 7 is hundredths, 5 is thousandths.
Adding/Subtracting Decimals: Line up the decimal points, then add or subtract as normal.
Multiplying Decimals: Multiply as whole numbers, then count total decimal places and insert point.
Dividing Decimals: Move the decimal point in divisor to make it a whole number; do the same for dividend.
Fraction to Decimal: Divide numerator by denominator. 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Always line up decimal points when adding or subtracting.
✅ Rule 2: When multiplying: count decimal places in BOTH numbers and put that many in the answer.
❌ Common Mistake: 0.3 × 0.3 ≠ 0.9. It equals 0.09 (2 decimal places in total).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Add 3.45 + 1.6

→ Line up: 3.45 + 1.60 = 5.05
Example 2: Multiply 2.4 × 1.5

→ 24 × 15 = 360; 2 decimal places → 3.60
Example 3: Convert 5/8 to decimal

→ 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625
