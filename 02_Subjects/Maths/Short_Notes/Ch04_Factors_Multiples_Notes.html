🔑 Key Concepts — Factors and Multiples

Factor: A number that divides exactly into another number. Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.
Multiple: The result of multiplying a number by a whole number. Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16...
Prime Number: A number with exactly two factors: 1 and itself. e.g. 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13.
HCF (Highest Common Factor): The largest factor shared by two or more numbers. HCF(12, 18) = 6.
LCM (Lowest Common Multiple): The smallest multiple shared by two or more numbers. LCM(4, 6) = 12.
Prime Factorisation: Expressing a number as a product of its prime factors. 12 = 2² × 3.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: 1 is NOT a prime number (only one factor). 2 is the only even prime number.
✅ Rule 2: To find HCF: list all factors of each number and pick the largest common one.
✅ Rule 3: To find LCM: list multiples of each number and pick the smallest common one.
❌ Common Mistake: Don't confuse HCF with LCM. HCF is smaller; LCM is larger (unless numbers are the same).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Find the factors of 24.

→ 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
Example 2: Find HCF(16, 24).

→ Factors of 16: 1,2,4,8,16. Factors of 24: 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24. Largest common = 8.
Example 3: Find LCM(3, 5).

→ Multiples of 3: 3,6,9,12,15... Multiples of 5: 5,10,15... Smallest common = 15.
