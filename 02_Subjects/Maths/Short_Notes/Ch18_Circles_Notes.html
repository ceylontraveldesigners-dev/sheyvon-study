🔑 Key Concepts — Circles

Radius (r): The distance from the centre of a circle to its edge.
Diameter (d): The distance across a circle through its centre. d = 2r.
Circumference: The perimeter (distance around) a circle. C = πd = 2πr.
Area of Circle: A = πr². The space inside the circle.
Pi (π): The constant ratio of circumference to diameter. π ≈ 3.14 or 22/7.
Arc: A part of the circumference (curved line).

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: Diameter = 2 × radius. Radius = diameter ÷ 2.
✅ Rule 2: Circumference = πd = 2πr. Area = πr².
❌ Common Mistake: Don't use the diameter in the area formula. Area = π × radius² (NOT diameter²).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Circumference of circle with radius 7 cm (use π = 22/7).

→ C = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 cm
Example 2: Area of circle with radius 5 cm (π = 3.14).

→ A = 3.14 × 5² = 3.14 × 25 = 78.5 cm²
Example 3: Diameter = 10 cm. Find radius and circumference.

→ r = 5 cm. C = π × 10 = 31.4 cm
