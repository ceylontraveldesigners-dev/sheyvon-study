🔑 Key Concepts — Ratios

Ratio: A comparison of two or more quantities. Written as a:b. e.g. 3:5
Simplifying Ratios: Divide both parts by their HCF. 12:8 → 3:2
Equivalent Ratios: Ratios with the same value when simplified. 2:3 = 4:6 = 6:9
Dividing in a Ratio: Total ÷ sum of parts × each part. Divide 40 in ratio 3:5 → 40÷8×3=15, 40÷8×5=25
Ratio to Fraction: a:b means a/(a+b) and b/(a+b) of the whole.

📌 Rules to Remember

✅ Rule 1: To simplify, divide both parts by their HCF.
✅ Rule 2: To divide a quantity in ratio a:b: sum parts (a+b), divide total by sum, multiply by each part.
❌ Common Mistake: 3:5 does NOT mean 3/5. It means 3 parts out of 8 (3/8 and 5/8).

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1: Simplify 18:24

→ HCF=6. 18÷6=3, 24÷6=4. Answer: 3:4
Example 2: Divide Rs.60 in ratio 2:3

→ Sum=5. 60÷5=12. Parts: 2×12=24, 3×12=36
Example 3: Are 4:6 and 6:9 equivalent?

→ 4:6→2:3; 6:9→2:3. Yes, equivalent.
