📌 Key Concepts

Temperature: Measure of how hot or cold something is. Measured in °C or Kelvin.
Heat: Form of energy that flows from hot to cold objects.
Conduction: Heat transfer through direct contact (mainly in solids).
Convection: Heat transfer through movement of fluids (liquids and gases).
Radiation: Heat transfer through electromagnetic waves — no medium needed. E.g. sunlight.
Thermometer: Instrument used to measure temperature.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Heat flows from hot objects to cold objects — never the reverse naturally.
✅ Conduction: heat transfers through contact (metals are good conductors).
✅ Convection: hot fluid rises, cool fluid sinks — forms convection currents.
✅ Radiation: heat travels as electromagnetic waves — can travel through vacuum.
✅ The Sun heats Earth by radiation — no medium needed.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Metal spoon in hot soup gets hot — conduction.
🔬 Hot air rising in a room — convection.
🔬 Feeling warmth from the Sun — radiation.
🔬 Sea breeze — convection current between land and sea.
