📌 Key Concepts

Umbra: The darkest part of a shadow, where all light is blocked.
Penumbra: The partial shadow around the umbra, where some light passes.
Plane Mirror: A flat mirror that produces a virtual, upright image.
Virtual Image: An image that cannot be projected on a screen — seen only in a mirror.
Concave Mirror: A curved mirror that curves inward; converges light; used in torches.
Convex Mirror: A curved mirror that curves outward; diverges light; used in car rear-view mirrors.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Light travels in straight lines.
✅ Angle of incidence = angle of reflection (Law of Reflection).
✅ Plane mirror image: virtual, upright, same size, laterally inverted, same distance behind mirror.
✅ Concave mirror can form real (inverted) or virtual (upright) images depending on object distance.
✅ Convex mirror always forms a virtual, upright, smaller image — used for wider field of view.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Solar eclipse — Moon forms an umbra and penumbra on Earth.
🔬 Torch reflector — concave mirror focuses light into a beam.
🔬 Car rear-view mirror — convex mirror gives wider view.
🔬 Looking in a flat bathroom mirror — plane mirror, virtual upright image.
