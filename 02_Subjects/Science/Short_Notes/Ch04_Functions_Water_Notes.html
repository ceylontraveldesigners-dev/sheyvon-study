📌 Key Concepts

Solvent: Water dissolves many substances — it is called the 'universal solvent'.
Coolant: Water absorbs heat to cool engines and bodies (sweating).
Medium of Life: Most living organisms need water to survive; reactions occur in water.
Solution: A mixture formed when a solute dissolves in a solvent (water).
Solute: The substance that dissolves in water.
Solubility: How much of a solute can dissolve in water at a given temperature.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Water is called the universal solvent because it dissolves more substances than any other liquid.
✅ Water as a coolant: used in car radiators and the human body (sweat).
✅ Water is essential as a medium for chemical reactions in living cells.
✅ Solubility increases with temperature for most solids.
✅ Sugar and salt are soluble in water; sand and oil are insoluble.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Dissolving sugar in tea — water acts as a solvent.
🔬 Car radiator uses water to prevent the engine overheating.
🔬 Sweating cools the body — water evaporates and carries heat away.
🔬 Blood (mostly water) transports nutrients and oxygen in the body.
