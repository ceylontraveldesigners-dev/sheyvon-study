📌 Key Concepts

Vertebrates: Animals with a backbone (vertebral column). Five classes.
Invertebrates: Animals WITHOUT a backbone. E.g. insects, worms, jellyfish.
Fish: Cold-blooded, gills, scales, live in water. E.g. tilapia, shark.
Amphibians: Cold-blooded, moist skin, live in water AND land. E.g. frog, toad.
Reptiles: Cold-blooded, dry scaly skin, lay eggs on land. E.g. crocodile, lizard.
Birds: Warm-blooded, feathers, wings, lay eggs. E.g. eagle, penguin.
Mammals: Warm-blooded, hair/fur, give birth to live young, suckle milk. E.g. human, bat.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Vertebrates: fish → amphibians → reptiles → birds → mammals (5 classes).
✅ Cold-blooded: body temperature changes with surroundings (fish, amphibians, reptiles).
✅ Warm-blooded: body temperature stays constant (birds, mammals).
✅ All mammals feed their young with milk from mammary glands.
✅ A dichotomous key uses paired questions to identify and classify organisms.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Frog — amphibian: starts life in water (tadpole), moves to land as adult.
🔬 Bat — mammal: warm-blooded, gives birth, feeds young with milk, has wings.
🔬 Penguin — bird: warm-blooded, feathers, cannot fly but swims well.
🔬 Snake — reptile: cold-blooded, dry scaly skin, lays eggs.
