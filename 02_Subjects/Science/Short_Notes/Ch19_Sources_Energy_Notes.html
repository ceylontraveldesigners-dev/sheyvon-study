📌 Key Concepts

Renewable Energy: Energy from sources that are naturally replenished. E.g. solar, wind, hydro.
Non-Renewable Energy: Energy from sources that cannot be replenished quickly. E.g. coal, oil, natural gas.
Solar Energy: Energy from the Sun captured by solar panels.
Wind Energy: Energy from moving air captured by wind turbines.
Hydroelectric Energy: Energy from moving water used to generate electricity.
Fossil Fuels: Coal, oil, and natural gas formed from ancient organisms over millions of years.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Renewable sources: solar, wind, hydroelectric, geothermal, biomass — will not run out.
✅ Non-renewable sources: coal, oil, natural gas — formed over millions of years; will run out.
✅ Burning fossil fuels releases CO₂ — major cause of global warming.
✅ Sustainable use means using energy in ways that can continue for future generations.
✅ Sri Lanka's main renewable source is hydroelectric power.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Solar panels on rooftops — convert sunlight to electricity.
🔬 Wind turbines in open areas — generate electricity from wind.
🔬 Hydroelectric dam — water flows through turbines to generate electricity.
🔬 Coal power station — burns coal (non-renewable) to produce steam to drive generators.
