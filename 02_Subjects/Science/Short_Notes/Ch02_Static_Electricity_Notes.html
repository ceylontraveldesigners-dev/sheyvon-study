📌 Key Concepts

Static Electricity: Build-up of electric charge on the surface of an object due to friction.
Positive Charge: Formed when electrons are removed from an object.
Negative Charge: Formed when electrons are added to an object.
Attraction: Opposite charges (+ and −) attract each other.
Repulsion: Like charges (+ and + or − and −) repel each other.
Capacitor: A device that stores electric charge.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Objects can be charged by rubbing (friction).
✅ Like charges repel; unlike charges attract.
✅ A charged object can attract small uncharged objects.
✅ Good conductors (metals) allow charge to flow easily.
✅ Insulators (rubber, plastic) do not allow charge to flow.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Rubbing a plastic ruler on hair — the ruler picks up electrons and becomes negatively charged.
🔬 Comb attracting small pieces of paper after combing hair.
🔬 Lightning — discharge of static electricity between clouds and earth.
🔬 Static shock when touching a metal doorknob.
