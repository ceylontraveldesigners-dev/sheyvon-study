📌 Key Concepts

Crust: The outermost solid layer of the Earth. Thinnest layer. We live on it.
Mantle: The thickest layer below the crust. Semi-molten rock (magma).
Outer Core: Liquid layer of iron and nickel surrounding the inner core.
Inner Core: The solid centre of the Earth. Extremely hot iron and nickel.
Tectonic Plates: Large pieces of the crust that move slowly on the mantle.
Plate Tectonics: Theory that Earth's crust is made of moving plates.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Earth has 4 layers: crust → mantle → outer core → inner core.
✅ The crust is thinnest; the mantle is thickest.
✅ The inner core is solid despite high temperature, due to extreme pressure.
✅ Tectonic plates move a few centimetres per year.
✅ Earthquakes and volcanoes occur mainly at plate boundaries.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Hawaii — volcanic islands formed at a hotspot in a tectonic plate.
🔬 Himalayan mountains — formed by collision of the Indian and Eurasian plates.
🔬 Earthquakes in Japan — Japan sits on multiple tectonic plate boundaries.
🔬 Mid-Atlantic Ridge — plates spreading apart on the ocean floor.
