📌 Key Concepts

Carbohydrates: Main energy source. Found in rice, bread, potatoes.
Proteins: For growth and repair. Found in meat, fish, eggs, beans.
Fats: Energy storage, insulation. Found in butter, oil, nuts.
Vitamins: Needed in small amounts for health. E.g. Vitamin C (fruits), Vitamin D (sunlight).
Minerals: Inorganic nutrients. E.g. calcium for bones, iron for blood.
Water: Essential for all body processes. Makes up about 60% of the body.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Carbohydrates, fats, and proteins provide energy. Protein also builds and repairs tissues.
✅ Iodine test: starch turns blue-black in iodine solution.
✅ Biuret test: protein turns purple/violet in biuret reagent (NaOH + CuSO₄).
✅ Ethanol test: fat — ethanol + water produces white emulsion.
✅ Benedict's test: glucose turns brick-red/orange when heated with Benedict's solution.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Iodine drops on rice — turns blue-black (starch present).
🔬 Boiled egg white + biuret reagent — turns purple (protein present).
🔬 Butter with ethanol and water — white emulsion (fat present).
🔬 Orange juice + Benedict's solution + heat — brick red (glucose present).
