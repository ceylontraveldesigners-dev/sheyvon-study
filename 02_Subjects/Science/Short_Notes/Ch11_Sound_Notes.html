📌 Key Concepts

Vibration: Sound is produced when objects vibrate back and forth.
Propagation: Sound travels through a medium (solid, liquid, gas) as a wave.
Frequency: Number of vibrations per second, measured in Hertz (Hz). Determines pitch.
Amplitude: Size of the vibration. Determines loudness of sound.
Pitch: How high or low a sound is. High frequency = high pitch.
Loudness: How loud or soft a sound is. Large amplitude = louder sound.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Sound needs a medium to travel — it cannot travel through a vacuum.
✅ Sound travels fastest in solids, slower in liquids, slowest in gases.
✅ High frequency = high pitch; low frequency = low pitch.
✅ Large amplitude = loud sound; small amplitude = soft sound.
✅ Sound frequency is measured in Hertz (Hz).

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Guitar string vibrating — produces sound; thinner strings vibrate faster (higher pitch).
🔬 Thunder — sound produced by rapid air vibration from lightning.
🔬 Tuning fork — produces a pure tone at a specific frequency.
🔬 Shouting in a room — large amplitude, loud sound.
