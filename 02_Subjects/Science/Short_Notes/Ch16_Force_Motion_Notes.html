📌 Key Concepts

Distance: Total length of path travelled. Scalar — has magnitude only.
Displacement: Shortest straight-line distance from start to finish. Vector — has magnitude AND direction.
Force: A push or pull that can change an object's motion, shape, or direction.
Gravity: A non-contact force that pulls objects towards each other (e.g. Earth pulling objects down).
Friction: A contact force that opposes motion between surfaces.
Magnetic Force: A non-contact force of attraction or repulsion between magnets or magnetic materials.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Distance is always positive; displacement can be positive, negative, or zero.
✅ Contact forces require physical contact (friction, tension, normal force).
✅ Non-contact forces act at a distance (gravity, magnetic, electrostatic).
✅ Friction opposes motion — it acts in the opposite direction to movement.
✅ The unit of force is the Newton (N).

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Pushing a door — contact force (applied force).
🔬 Earth's gravity pulling an apple down — non-contact force.
🔬 Friction between shoes and floor — prevents slipping.
🔬 A magnet attracting iron filings — non-contact magnetic force.
