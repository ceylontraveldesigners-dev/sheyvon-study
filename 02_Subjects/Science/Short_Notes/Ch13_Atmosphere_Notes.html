📌 Key Concepts

Troposphere: Lowest layer. Contains weather. We breathe here. 0–12 km.
Stratosphere: Contains ozone layer. Absorbs UV rays. 12–50 km.
Mesosphere: Meteors burn up here. 50–80 km.
Thermosphere: Very hot. Aurora borealis occurs here. 80–700 km.
Exosphere: Outermost layer. Merges with space. Satellites orbit here.
Ozone Layer: Found in stratosphere. Protects Earth from harmful UV radiation.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Air is approximately 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.04% carbon dioxide, and small amounts of other gases.
✅ The troposphere is where all weather occurs.
✅ The ozone layer in the stratosphere absorbs harmful UV radiation from the Sun.
✅ Meteors burn up in the mesosphere due to friction with air particles.
✅ CFCs (from aerosols) damage the ozone layer.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Weather (rain, wind) — occurs in troposphere.
🔬 Ozone layer in stratosphere absorbs UV — without it, UV rays would harm life.
🔬 Shooting stars — meteors burning in the mesosphere.
🔬 Northern Lights (aurora) — occurs in thermosphere.
