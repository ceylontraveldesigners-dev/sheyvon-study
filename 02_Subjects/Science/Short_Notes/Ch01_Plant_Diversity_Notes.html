📌 Key Concepts

Flowering Plants: Plants that produce flowers and fruits. Seeds are enclosed in a fruit.
Non-Flowering Plants: Plants that do not produce flowers, e.g. ferns, mosses, cycas.
Monocots: One seed leaf (cotyledon). Parallel leaf veins. E.g. rice, coconut, grass.
Dicots: Two seed leaves. Net-like leaf veins. E.g. mango, bean, rose.
Tap Root System: One main root with smaller branches. E.g. carrot, rose.
Fibrous Root System: Many roots of similar size. E.g. rice, grass.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Flowering plants have roots, stems, leaves, flowers, fruits and seeds.
✅ Monocots have parallel venation; dicots have reticulate (net) venation.
✅ Tap roots grow deep; fibrous roots spread widely near the surface.
✅ Non-flowering plants reproduce by spores, not seeds.
✅ Plants make food by photosynthesis using sunlight, water, and CO₂.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Rose — flowering, dicot, tap root, reticulate venation.
🔬 Rice — flowering, monocot, fibrous root, parallel venation.
🔬 Fern — non-flowering, reproduces by spores.
🔬 Coconut — monocot; one large seed leaf inside the coconut.
