📌 Key Concepts

Kinetic Energy: Energy of moving objects. E.g. rolling ball, blowing wind.
Potential Energy: Stored energy due to position or condition. E.g. stretched rubber band, water in a dam.
Electrical Energy: Energy carried by electric current. E.g. powering lights and fans.
Sound Energy: Energy from vibrating objects. E.g. music, thunder.
Light Energy: Energy from electromagnetic waves. E.g. sunlight, torchlight.
Heat/Thermal Energy: Energy due to temperature. E.g. fire, hot water.
Chemical Energy: Stored in chemical bonds. Released by burning or in batteries.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Energy cannot be created or destroyed — it can only be converted from one form to another.
✅ Kinetic energy increases with speed and mass.
✅ Potential energy is stored; kinetic energy is due to motion.
✅ All energy transformations have some energy lost as heat.
✅ The Sun is the primary source of energy on Earth.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Light bulb: electrical energy → light energy + heat energy.
🔬 Burning wood: chemical energy → heat energy + light energy.
🔬 Waterfall: potential energy → kinetic energy → electrical energy (hydroelectric).
🔬 Loudspeaker: electrical energy → sound energy.
