📌 Key Concepts

Chemical Energy: Batteries convert chemical energy to electrical energy.
Mechanical Energy: Generators convert kinetic (moving) energy to electrical energy.
Solar Energy: Solar panels convert light energy to electrical energy.
Direct Current (DC): Current flows in ONE direction only. E.g. batteries.
Alternating Current (AC): Current changes direction repeatedly. E.g. mains electricity.
Simple Circuit: Path through which current flows: cell, wires, bulb, switch.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Electricity can be generated from chemical, mechanical, solar, wind, and hydro sources.
✅ DC flows in one direction; AC alternates direction many times per second.
✅ A complete circuit is needed for current to flow.
✅ Generators convert kinetic energy into electrical energy.
✅ Solar panels use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight to electricity.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 AA battery — chemical energy → electrical energy (DC).
🔬 Hydroelectric dam — kinetic energy of water → electrical energy.
🔬 Solar calculator — light energy → electrical energy.
🔬 Wind turbine — kinetic energy of wind → electrical energy.
