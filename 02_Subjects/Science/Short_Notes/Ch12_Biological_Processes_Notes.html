📌 Key Concepts

Cell: The basic unit of life. All living things are made of cells.
Tissue: A group of similar cells working together. E.g. muscle tissue.
Organ: A group of tissues working together. E.g. heart, lungs.
Organ System: A group of organs working together. E.g. digestive system.
Organism: A complete living thing made of organ systems.
Digestive System: Breaks down food into nutrients the body can absorb.

📏 Key Rules & Facts

✅ Organisation: cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism.
✅ The human body has many systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory, etc.
✅ Each system has a specific function essential for life.
✅ Cells are the smallest unit that can carry out life processes.
✅ The circulatory system transports blood, oxygen, and nutrients.

🔬 Real-Life Examples

🔬 Heart — an organ made of cardiac muscle tissue.
🔬 Digestive system: mouth → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine.
🔬 Respiratory system: breathing in oxygen and releasing CO₂.
🔬 Nervous system: brain and nerves control the body.
